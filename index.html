A note from Anna

Slow craft, started in 2013 on a rainy afternoon in Devon.

What began as a blog — a small French voice writing about knitting from her new home in England — is now a studio, a yarn range, and a shop on New Bridge Street in Exeter, where we open the doors most days of the week.

We make patterns we want to knit ourselves. We choose yarns from spinning mills who treat the people, the animals and the land as carefully as we treat the wool itself. Nothing is rushed. Most things take a season to make properly. We like it that way.

— Anna

The yarn

Where it comes from, who made it, why it matters.

We work with spinning mills that respect animals, humans, and the land they share — every base we sell is fully traceable.

  • OriginSouth African mohair from farms where mulesing is not practiced.
  • Silk baseCertified cruelty-free (peace silk) — no boiled cocoons.
  • WoolSelected blends from spinning mills working to ethical and environmental guidelines.
  • DyeHand-dyed in small batches at the studio — colours vary slightly from skein to skein, on purpose.
  • QuantitiesWe make only what we can sell. When a colourway sells out, we dye another bath rather than over-print.
